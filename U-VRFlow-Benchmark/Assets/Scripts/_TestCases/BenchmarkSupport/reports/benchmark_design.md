# Integrated Nine-Category Benchmark

The active benchmark is organized by the primary semantic control variable:

1. CoreDataflow
2. ObjectIdentityHeap
3. UnityLifecycle
4. AsyncTemporal
5. DynamicInvocation
6. RuntimeEventDispatch
7. ConfigurationRecoveredEdges
8. Composite
9. Privacy

The manifest contains one row per logical case. Multi-file owner/target and writer/reader cases share one case id. `legacy_case_id` and `legacy_category` preserve traceability. Configuration facts are derived benchmark inputs and do not define labels.
